What is Scarlet iOS? A complete guide to the IPA installer

Scarlet iOS is a free third-party app installer for iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch. It lets you sideload .ipa files onto your device without jailbreaking, so you can install apps and tweaks that are not on the official App Store. It also handles repos, backups, and certificate management.

What is Scarlet iOS?

Scarlet iOS is an app that installs other apps. Instead of downloading only from Apple's App Store, you can load an app file called an .ipa and install it straight onto your phone. This process is known as sideloading. Scarlet acts like a small, private app store that sits on your device.

The tool exists because the App Store does not list every app. Some developers share builds outside of Apple's review process, and some apps are modified versions of ones you already know. Scarlet gives you a place to install and manage those files. If you want to grab the current build, you can download Scarlet iOS from the official source and follow the on-screen steps.

How Scarlet iOS works

Scarlet signs an .ipa file with a certificate and then installs it on your device. The certificate is what tells iOS that the app is allowed to run. Scarlet supports a few sources for this, including your own Apple ID or an imported developer certificate. Once the app is installed, you trust its profile in Settings and open it like any normal app.

Because Scarlet does not touch the core system files, your device stays in its normal state. There is no jailbreak involved. That keeps the setup simple and reversible. If you ever want the app gone, you delete it and remove its profile.

Supported devices and iOS versions

Scarlet runs on Apple devices with iOS 12 or newer. That range is wide, so most phones from the last several years will work.

  • iPhone: iPhone 8 series through the iPhone 16 series
  • iPad: modern iPad, iPad Pro, iPad Air, and iPad mini models
  • iOS: versions 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 18

The app is lightweight, so it does not take much storage. Signing and installing an app usually takes under a minute on a modern device.

Who should use Scarlet iOS?

Scarlet fits people who want more control over what they install. Developers use it to test builds and inject tweaks. Regular users try it for apps that never reached the App Store. If you like customizing how your apps look and behave, the repo and tweak features are the main draw.

One thing to keep in mind is source safety. A sideloaded app is only as trustworthy as the file behind it. Stick to files from sources you know, and avoid random links. Scarlet itself does not support piracy, so only install apps that are free or that you already own.

Is Scarlet iOS free?

Yes. The installer is free and there is no paid unlock for the core features. You do not pay to sideload, to add a repo, or to back up an app. The only cost you might run into is optional, such as buying a paid Apple developer certificate for longer signing periods, but that is not required to get started.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scarlet iOS free to use?

Yes. Scarlet iOS is free to download and use. There is no paid tier for the core installer, and you do not need a subscription to sideload .ipa files.

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone to use Scarlet?

No. Scarlet works on non-jailbroken devices. It installs apps through sideloading and does not change the core iOS system files.

Which devices does Scarlet iOS support?

Scarlet supports iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch running iOS 12 or newer, which covers iPhone 8 through iPhone 16 and modern iPad models.

What file format does Scarlet install?

Scarlet installs apps packaged as .ipa files. It also supports importing .deb or dylib files to tweak apps you have already sideloaded.
